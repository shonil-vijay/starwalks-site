StarWalks

Walk to catch the stars.

Stars are scattered along the real walkable paths around you. Start a walk, stroll up to one, and it's yours. The more you move, the more of your neighborhood's sky you collect.

Capture by walking. Deterministic stars sit on real sidewalks and paths — reach one on foot to catch it.
Track your progress. Steps, distance, and streaks by day, week, month, and year, saved to Apple Health as workouts.
Walk with friends. Private group leaderboards compare daily totals — never your location or routes.
Yours, and private. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location never leaves your device.
